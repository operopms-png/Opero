Live Dashboard

See properties, cleaning tasks, maintenance tickets, and revenue at a glance. Real-time data from your Supabase database.

🏠

Property Management

Add and manage unlimited properties with address, bedrooms, bathrooms, guest capacity, and status tracking.

📅

Bookings

Track all bookings with guest details, check-in/out dates, revenue, platform source, and status management.

🧹

Cleaning Tasks

Schedule and track cleaning tasks per property. Assign cleaners, set dates, add notes, and update status in real time.

🔄

Turnovers

Schedule property turnovers with check-out and check-in times, assign staff, and track completion status.

🔧

Maintenance Tickets

Log issues with priority levels, assign contractors, track resolution status, and keep full repair history per property.

Analytics

Occupancy gauge, revenue charts, booking trends, and monthly breakdowns — all calculated from your live booking data.

Reports

Revenue by month, revenue by property, total bookings, and KPI cards — a full financial overview of your portfolio.

Owner Reports

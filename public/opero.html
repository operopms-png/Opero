FAQ

Questions, answered

What's included in Vacation Rentals (STR)?

Bookings, CRM, cleaning and maintenance scheduling, banking and reports, guest messaging, a contractor portal, and direct iCal sync with Airbnb and Vrbo — everything you need to run short-term rentals in one place.

What does Property Management cover?

Tenants and leases, rent collection, inspections, an owner reporting portal, and full banking and reports — built for landlords and managers running long-term lets.

What's in the Estate Agency module?

Vacancy management, rent collection, a landlord portal, a built-in CRM, and banking and reports — designed for agencies managing properties on behalf of landlords.

What does the Developments module do?

Track projects and budgets, manage investors, hit milestones, and coordinate contractors and documents — purpose-built for property developers managing builds from start to finish.

What can I do with the Invest (Deal Analyser) module?

Run the numbers on a property before you buy. Eight investment strategies are covered — BTL, HMO, BRRR, R2R, Social Housing, Supported Living, Flip & Land — plus a watchlist, saved deals, and an ROI calculator.

Can I use multiple modules together, e.g. STR and PM?

Yes. Every module shares one login and one dashboard family, so you can run Vacation Rentals and Property Management side by side without juggling separate logins or tools. Add the bundle for all six modules at a 10% discount.

Can I use just one module?

Yes. Every module — Vacation Rentals, Property Management, Estate Agency, Developments and Invest — can be activated on its own. Add more later as your business grows.

Is there a contract or minimum term?

No. Opero is billed monthly with no lock-in. Cancel any time from your account settings.

Do you take a commission on bookings?

No. Your subscription price is fixed — we don't take a cut of your booking revenue or rent collected.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or add/remove modules at any time. Changes apply from your next billing cycle.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export your bookings, tenants, and financial records at any time. We retain your data for a limited period after cancellation in case you want to return, then it's deleted.

Is the AI Property Manager fully autonomous?

AI Property Manager is an actively developing feature. Today it gives you a central place to manage and log AI-assisted workflows across guest communication, maintenance, cleaning, pricing, owner reporting and lead qualification — with deeper automation rolling out over time.
